# Suggested Overleaf Gallery entry

## Title

Global Science Today official article template

## Description

Official LaTeX manuscript template for Global Science Today (GST), an
international, peer-reviewed, open-access multidisciplinary journal published
by Horizon e-Publishing Group. The template supports Research Articles, Short
Communications, Review Articles and Mini-review Articles across the journal's
subject areas. It includes journal metadata, author affiliations and ORCIDs,
abstract and keywords, Vancouver-style numbered references, figures, tables,
ethical declarations, data availability, funding, author contributions and
author biographies.

Author guidelines:
https://globalsciencetoday.com/index.php/gst/about/submissions

Journal website:
https://globalsciencetoday.com/index.php/gst
